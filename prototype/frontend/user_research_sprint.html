🚀 EdTech User Research Sprint

4-Week Accelerated Action Plan for Career-Switching App

⚡ Week 1: Foundation Setup & Initial Interviews (Days 1-7)

🎯 Days 1-2: Quick Setup & Immediate Outreach

Infrastructure (Complete in 4 hours)
  • Set up Calendly with 20 and 45-minute slots
  • Set up Miro journey mapping template
  • Install analytics on demo
  • Create interview guide template
Immediate User Recruitment (Complete by Day 2)
  • Post in 3 Reddit subs simultaneously (r/careerguidance, r/careerchange, r/ITCareerQuestions)
  • Join 3 Facebook career change groups and post immediately
  • Message 10 people in your network for referrals
  • Post on personal social media asking for career switcher connections

🎤 Days 3-7: First Wave Interviews (Target: 8-10 interviews)

Quick Interview Strategy
  • Book interviews within 24-48 hours of contact
  • Offer same-day/next-day slots for eager participants
  • Mix: 3 currently unhappy workers, 3 successful switchers, 2 failed/stalled switchers
  • Keep to 20 minutes max to get more yes responses
  • Record everything and take minimal notes during calls

💡 Power Questions (Ask these in EVERY interview):

  • "What's the single biggest thing stopping you from switching careers right now?"
  • "Walk me through your last attempt to learn a new skill - what happened?"
  • "If I could solve one problem for career switchers, what should it be?"
🔍 Week 2: Demo Testing + Journey Mapping (Days 8-14)

🖥️ Days 8-10: Rapid Demo Testing (Target: 8 users, 30 min each)

  • Recruit directly from Week 1 interviewees (they're already warmed up)
  • Use screen sharing, ask them to think aloud
  • Focus only on: What confuses them? Where do they drop off? What excites them?
  • Synthesize findings after each day

🗺️ Days 11-14: Condensed Journey Mapping (Target: 4 sessions, 45 min each)

  • Select best 4 users from previous interviews
  • Use Miro to map their career switch journey in real-time
  • Focus on: emotional low points, resource gaps, moments they almost quit
  • Identify the 2-3 most critical "bridge" moments your app should address
🏆 Week 3: Competitive Intelligence + Real-World Context (Days 15-21)

⚔️ Days 15-17: Speed Competitor Analysis

  • Pick 2 main competitors (not 4)
  • Recruit 4 users, give them $30 each (lower amount, faster commitment)
  • Give them 3 days max to try competitors
  • 15-minute debrief calls focusing on: "What did they do better than current solutions?"
  • Look for gaps, not features to copy

📚 Days 18-21: Learning Context Deep-Dive

  • Ask 6 users to document 3 days of learning attempts (not a full week)
  • Focus on: What time of day? What device? What interrupts them? What motivates continuation?
  • Text/WhatsApp check-ins instead of formal surveys
  • Quick 10-minute wrap-up calls
🎯 Week 4: Synthesis + Validation (Days 22-28)

🔬 Days 22-24: Data Synthesis Sprint

  • Block out 6 hours to analyze all interviews
  • Create 2-3 user personas maximum
  • Identify top 3 pain points that emerged across ALL research
  • Map these to potential app features/solutions

✅ Days 25-26: Rapid Validation

  • Create simple landing page or survey with your key hypotheses
  • Send to all research participants + broader demo user base
  • Ask direct questions: "Would you pay $X for this solution?" "How often would you use this?"
  • Get responses within 48 hours

🚀 Days 27-28: Strategy & Next Steps

  • Define your minimum viable feature set based on research
  • Prioritize development roadmap
  • Plan user acquisition strategy based on what worked in recruitment
  • Set up ongoing feedback loop with 5-8 power users
⚡ ACCELERATION TACTICS

🏃‍♂️ Recruitment Shortcuts

  • Offer higher incentives for faster response ($30-40 vs $25)
  • Use existing demo users as referral sources
  • Post in multiple channels simultaneously
  • Accept phone interviews if people prefer

🔍 Research Shortcuts

  • Combine interview types (ask journey questions during demo testing)
  • Use templates and standardized questions religiously
  • Record everything, transcribe later with AI tools
  • Focus on patterns after every 3-4 interviews
📊 SUCCESS METRICS & RED FLAGS

✅ Weekly Success Indicators

Week 1 Success:
  • 8+ interviews completed
  • 20+ new demo users
  • 3 clear pain points identified
Week 2 Success:
  • 8+ demo tests completed
  • 4 journey maps completed
  • Clear understanding of where your app fits in user workflow
Week 3 Success:
  • Competitor gaps identified
  • Learning context patterns clear
  • Feature priority list drafted
Week 4 Success:
  • User personas defined
  • MVP features validated
  • Next 30-day development plan ready

🚩 Red Flags to Watch For

  • Users say "it's cool but I don't have time"
  • Can't identify when/where they'd use your app
  • Learning method doesn't match their preferences
  • Time commitment unrealistic for their lifestyle
  • Can't see clear path from your app to career success
